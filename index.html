Frequently Asked Questions

What ages does Echelon Day Care accept?

We accept children from 30 months to 5 years old. Our programs prepare children for a smooth transition to kindergarten.

Where are you located?

We are at 575 W 8th Ave, Vancouver, BC V5Z 1M9. You can find us on the embedded map on our Contact page.

How do I join the waiting list?

Fill out the online waiting list form on our Services page and we will get back to you as soon as a spot opens.

Is Echelon Day Care licensed?

Yes, Echelon Day Care is a licensed childcare provider operating in Vancouver, British Columbia.

What does a typical day look like?

Our day is play-based and structured with circle time, indoor learning centres, outdoor play, art, music, story time and rest periods. Our Virtual Tour page shows the spaces where this happens.

How can I get in touch?

Call or text +1 604-874-4010, email echelondaycare@hotmail.com, or use the contact form. We typically respond within one business day.
